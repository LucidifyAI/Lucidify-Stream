Open this page from Lucid-Stream

Tap Listen in the Lucid-Stream app on this computer. The app opens this page with a one-time link.

Before you start

This page connects to the Lucid-Stream app running on this computer. Your browser may ask to connect to devices on your local network or apps on this device. Choose Allow.

Your EEG stays on this computer. It goes from the app to this page and is turned into sound here.

Connection blocked by the browser

To allow it, click the icon left of the address bar, open Site settings, and set Local network access (or Apps on device) to Allow. Then reload this page.
